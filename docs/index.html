Community & Support

This is still very much a toy compiler and a work in progress. It is in the early stages of development, but still developed enough to share to a public. It’s not yet production-ready by any means, but that’s exactly why your help is needed!

Contributions and testing are the best ways to help. Whether it’s reporting bugs, submitting code, or sharing ideas, every bit of support would be greatly appreciated.
